# Skill: Premium Landing Art Direction

Use this skill for landing pages that need conversion strength and high visual impact.

## Core Directive

Build the page as a persuasive product story, not a list of sections. Every block must answer a
specific visitor question and move them toward action.

## Landing Structure

- Hero: sharp promise, specific audience, one dominant CTA, one visual proof element.
- Problem tension: name the pain or missed opportunity in concrete language.
- Mechanism: explain how the product/service creates the outcome.
- Proof: metrics, use cases, testimonials, logos, screenshots, or before/after framing.
- Offer: package the value clearly, with what is included and why it matters now.
- Objection handling: address trust, cost, time, risk, setup, or support.
- Final CTA: repeat the strongest action with lower-friction language.

## Visual Direction

- Use a confident above-the-fold composition: split-stage, editorial poster, product cockpit, or cinematic panel.
- Give CTAs physical presence with contrast, spacing, and directional support.
- Use section transitions that feel designed: overlap, rhythm shift, color field, or narrative reveal.
- Use real-sounding copy and concrete nouns. Avoid generic claims like "innovative solution".

## Output Checks

- A visitor can understand the offer in 5 seconds.
- The page has one primary conversion path.
- At least three proof signals are visible before the final CTA.
- The design feels campaign-ready, not template-default.
