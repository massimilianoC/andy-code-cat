# website-information-architecture

Applies to: `presetIds: website`, `tags: company, services, multi-section`

Use this skill when generating a one-page website or institutional site.

## Goal

The artifact must answer who the organization is, what it offers, why it is credible, and how
to start a relationship.

## Required Structure

- Header with logo/name, anchor navigation, and contact or CTA.
- Hero with identity, audience, and value proposition.
- About or positioning section.
- Services/products section with cards.
- Process/method section.
- Proof section: portfolio, cases, testimonials, partners, credentials, or gallery.
- Contact section with form/contact details.
- Footer with practical links and identity details.

## Navigation Rules

- Every navigation item must target a real section anchor.
- Section headings must be descriptive.
- Do not hide essential contact information in the footer only.

## Content Rules

- Services should say who they help and what outcome they produce.
- About text should establish trust, not become biography filler.
- Proof should be concrete or clearly replaceable.

## Acceptance Checks

- The site answers "who", "what", "why trust", and "how to contact".
- Header navigation works in-page.
- Contact path is visible before the footer.
- The page remains useful without JavaScript.
